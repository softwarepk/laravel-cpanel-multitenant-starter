# General rules

- Preserve the established Laravel/Livewire/Flux stack unless a concrete requirement justifies a change.
- Prefer Laravel-native features and existing starter patterns.
- Keep the starter generic: no project-specific business entities, terminology, or workflows.
- Do not add billing, subscriptions, teams, SaaS plans, Redis, Horizon, or external identity providers without an explicit requirement.
- Keep cPanel/Linux deployment as a first-class production target.
